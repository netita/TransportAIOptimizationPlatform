================================================================
DISCORDIA AI OPERATIONS PLATFORM — DEMO GUIDE
================================================================
Audience : Discordia management / stakeholders
Duration : 20–30 minutes
URL       : http://localhost:3000
----------------------------------------------------------------


BEFORE YOU START
----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Run in terminal:
   docker ps --filter name=discordia --format "{{.Names}}: {{.Status}}"

   All 7 containers must show "healthy" or "Up":
   - discordia-frontend
   - discordia-backend
   - discordia-postgres
   - discordia-redis
   - discordia-ml
   - discordia-zookeeper
   - discordia-simulator

2. Open http://localhost:3000 in a browser (full screen)

3. Keep a terminal open in the background for live demos (optional)

NOTE: All data is simulated but realistic — same corridors, same
border crossings, same warehouse hubs as real Discordia operations.
In production, the simulator is replaced by a live OTM/GPS feed.


================================================================
PART 1 — LIVE DASHBOARD (5 minutes)
================================================================

WHAT TO SAY:
"This is the Discordia AI Operations Platform. Everything you see
is updating in real time — trucks are moving, border wait times
are changing, and the AI is continuously analyzing the fleet."

WHAT TO SHOW:

1. KPI CARDS (top row)
   - Point to: Active Shipments, Delayed, At-Risk, Trucks Online
   - "These update automatically. No refresh needed."
   - Point to the green "Live" dot in the header
   - "That means we have a live connection to the data feed."

2. LIVE FLEET MAP
   - "50 trucks across Europe — GPS positions updating every 5 seconds"
   - Point to orange pulsing dots: "Orange means the AI flagged
     an anomaly — unusual speed, no GPS signal, suspicious pattern"
   - Point to the corridor lines on the map:
     "These are our 4 main trade corridors — Turkey to Germany,
     Bucharest to Berlin, Sofia to Vienna, Varna to Hamburg"

3. ALERTS FEED (right side)
   - "Operational alerts sorted by severity — border incidents,
     SLA risks, anomalies — all in one place"

4. WAREHOUSE & BORDER PANELS
   - "Live dock availability and queue times at all 8 hubs"
   - "Live wait times at all 8 European border crossings"


================================================================
PART 2 — SHIPMENT INTELLIGENCE (5 minutes)
================================================================

WHAT TO SAY:
"Let me show you what the AI knows about each individual shipment."

WHAT TO SHOW:

1. Click "View All" on the Shipments table
   OR navigate to http://localhost:3000/shipments

2. Point to the ML columns on the right:
   - "AI Delay Prob — XGBoost machine learning model predicts
     probability of delay for every active shipment"
   - "Predicted Delay — how many minutes late, per shipment"
   - "These models retrain automatically every 30 minutes
     on fresh live data"

3. Click on any shipment row with a high risk score (red bar)
   This opens the SHIPMENT DETAIL PAGE

4. On the detail page, walk through each section:

   ROUTE MAP
   - "Green dot is origin, red dot is destination"
   - "Blue square is the assigned truck's last GPS position"
   - "The dashed line is the planned route"

   AI PREDICTION
   - "94% delay probability — the model is almost certain
     this shipment will be late"
   - "Top factors: border inspection rate, wait time,
     warehouse congestion — these are the exact causes"

   ROUTE OPTIMIZATION
   - "The system compared all available corridors using
     live border data and recommends switching here"
   - Point to the border breakdown with red bottlenecks:
     "Kalotina: 1512 minutes — that's the problem"

   DISPATCHER ACTIONS
   - "Specific recommended actions for this shipment —
     not generic advice, tied to this exact shipment's data"


================================================================
PART 3 — DISPATCHER RECOMMENDATION SYSTEM (3 minutes)
================================================================

WHAT TO SAY:
"The platform doesn't just show data — it tells your dispatchers
exactly what to do, ranked by urgency and impact."

WHAT TO SHOW:

1. Go back to http://localhost:3000 (main dashboard)
2. Scroll down to the DISPATCHER ACTIONS panel
3. Walk through the action types:

   REROUTE  — "Corridor switch saves 2+ hours. One click decision."
   ESCALATE — "ML predicts 90%+ delay on a CRITICAL shipment.
               Needs a phone call now."
   INVESTIGATE — "Anomaly detector flagged this truck.
                  Something is wrong — check it."
   HOLD     — "Warehouse at 90% congestion. Don't send
               more trucks until it clears."

4. Point to the urgency badges:
   "CRITICAL items are always at the top. Dispatcher sees
   the most important thing first, every time."


================================================================
PART 4 — AI COPILOT LIVE DEMO (8 minutes)
================================================================

WHAT TO SAY:
"This is the AI Copilot — powered by Claude, Anthropic's AI.
It has live access to the entire fleet: GPS, shipments,
borders, warehouses, ML predictions, anomalies."

HOW TO OPEN: Click the blue bot icon (bottom-right corner)

--- QUESTION 1: Morning Briefing ---
Type: "Give me a full operational briefing for the morning shift"

WHAT TO SAY while it loads:
"Watch — it pulls data from 6 different sources simultaneously
and gives a single operational summary."

EXPECTED ANSWER: Active count, delayed list, border alerts,
warehouse congestion, top recommended actions.

--- QUESTION 2: Live Border Comparison ---
Type: "Is the Serbia route or the Romania route faster for
Istanbul to Munich right now?"

WHAT TO SAY:
"This is the key question dispatchers face every day.
Watch how it answers."

EXPECTED ANSWER: Exact border wait times per crossing,
calculated corridor totals, clear recommendation with reason.

WHAT TO POINT OUT:
"It said Kalotina: 1512 minutes. That is the live number
from our border data feed right now — not a generic answer."

--- QUESTION 3: SLA Risk ---
Type: "Which shipments will miss SLA today?"

WHAT TO SAY:
"Connect this directly to money — every missed SLA is a
penalty. The AI identifies them before they happen."

--- QUESTION 4: Scenario Planning ---
Type: "What would happen if Kapitan Andreevo border closes
for 4 hours?"

WHAT TO SAY:
"This is planning intelligence — not just monitoring.
How many shipments affected, which alternative corridors,
estimated delay per shipment."

--- INVITE THE BOSS TO ASK SOMETHING ---
Say: "Ask it anything about the fleet right now."

This works well because:
- People always ask something the demo didn't cover
- The Copilot almost always gives a useful answer
- It shows the system is not scripted

================================================================
IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
================================================================

Header shows "Connecting" instead of "Live":
   Wait 10 seconds — it reconnects automatically.
   If it persists: docker compose restart backend

Copilot gives a generic answer:
   Rephrase to be more specific:
   "Which border has the highest wait time right now?"
   instead of "How are the borders?"

Map not loading on shipment detail:
   Hard refresh: Ctrl+Shift+R

Backend error / blank page:
   docker compose restart backend
   Wait 15 seconds, refresh browser.


================================================================
QUESTIONS THE BOSS MIGHT ASK
================================================================

Q: Is this real data?
A: "Simulated — but same routes, borders, and hubs as real
   Discordia operations. Production connects to OTM and GPS."

Q: How long to connect to our real systems?
A: "The OTM integration layer is the next development phase.
   The platform is designed for it from day one."

Q: What happens if the AI makes a wrong recommendation?
A: "Every recommendation shows its reasoning — the dispatcher
   always makes the final decision. The AI surfaces the right
   information faster, it does not act autonomously."

Q: How accurate is the delay prediction?
A: "MAE of 3.9 minutes — predictions are within 4 minutes
   of actual delay on average. Retrained every 30 minutes
   on fresh data."

Q: Can we add more corridors or border crossings?
A: "Yes — corridors and borders are configurable. Adding a
   new corridor is a data change, not a code change."
